2. **Second law** states force is mass multiplied by acceleration: F = m·a. A large mass has small acceleration for a given force, and a small mass has large acceleration for a given force.

3. **Third law** states that for every action, a reaction exists. A push on a wall has a wall push back on a pusher.

A law is a general fact, not a specific fact about a body. Bodies with mass can change direction (as in circular motion) and speed without a force, as long as a force acts on it.
